# Native action dispatch checkpoint

The production CLI does not install this reader. The OSCAR action option stays
disabled by default. This checkpoint is for source review and operator
qualification. Deployment needs the matching A2 journal schema and A3 native
admission, followed by B1/B2 consumer commissioning. Release pins and CI
configuration are unchanged.

## Firing member identity and entry

The app adapter binds the A3 journal. It plans native routes with immutable
source labels and the effective severity view. A publication updates grouping,
inhibition sources and the local list view. The outgoing member retains its
provider pointer and exact expiry even when the notification copy freezes its
firing status. The group store keys members by the original provider identity.
Projected nflog hashes distinguish two source alerts with identical effective
labels.

The first verified private OSCAR callback enrolls the native identity. Every
tracked firing contact reserves episode and destination coverage. Explicit work
is selected by a durable operation ID. Its pending receiver, integration and
scope are revalidated on claim. Narrow notifier scopes can reach only the
canonical callback. Its signed delivery ID is derived from the original
operation and target. B2 must validate the signed scope, authorize each
downstream notifier and coalesce its individual receipt with ordinary fan-out.

The selected pipeline reads the group's baseline for managed eligibility and
sends only the selected member. It leaves the ordinary group log and clocks
alone. A successful native webhook response confirms transport acceptance.
B2 owns downstream notification and ticket results. An ambiguous response
keeps its entry held. The sender disables redirect following and request-body
replay for an entered webhook. Recovery may consume a still-pending target.
Fresh policy authority for a hold, expiry or successor comes from C3.

The ordinary reader stores a bounded membership summary of the owner's
recovered sources. A positive bit only selects the exact indexed journal
lookup. A missing candidate can obtain a local pass for its exact provider
pointer and live epoch. Source enrollment and direct entry compete under the
same local map guard. An uncertain direct entry retains its local fence. A
never-enrolled contact has no durable pre-entry row after process loss. A replacement owner retains ordinary native failover behavior for contacts made before identity enrollment. A later independently eligible contact can overlap a pre-enrollment contact whose result died with the owner. Cross-owner exclusion starts at durable enrollment.

## Graph and qualification limits

Action transport validation can distinguish a verified canonical private
callback from a direct native receiver. The managed conversion producer retains
its separate commissioning predicate. The adapter's source checkpoint
supports literal webhook destinations. File-based or templated destinations
and non-webhook integrations still need a qualified final-entry boundary.
Some SDKs and protocols perform additional operations inside a single notifier
invocation.

Tracked ordinary resolution uses the private `reserve-terminal` command after
normal group eligibility, send-resolved filtering and webhook truncation.
The journal derives the operation ID from the retained episode row, variant,
terminal sequence and destination. Claim rechecks current terminal identity,
the exact original source pointer and expiry, routing, suppression and the
lease. An entered target keeps its receipt after re-fire. A canonical callback
carries a stable delivery UUID and ordinary lifecycle semantics. An independent
firing action cannot use a terminal claim. The claim does not advance a repeat
or reserve firing coverage.

At takeover the native owner pages firing projections and distinct retained
source keys. Each recovery turn pins its lease epoch. The first turn of a new epoch marks resident projections uncertain before recording any page cursor. A firing slot omitted after another owner closed it must refresh its exact journal row. Page cursors resume after transient failures within their epoch. Each turn
reads at most 100 firing projections and 64 distinct source keys, irrespective
of each key's historical interval count. Ordinary entry requires complete
recovery. A conservative membership hit selects the new indexed exact-interval
lookup. The matching A2 schema upgrade and private lease marker
`terminal_observations: 2` are required.

Selection preserves ordinary input order. The native retry stage first applies
send-resolved, then the action reader fixes the webhook prefix before any
identity enrollment or reservation. The group log retains ordinary membership
after a successful truncated send. A limited batch with an unverifiable input waits for a fresh group. A direct sibling accepted beside a covered member enters the ordinary group log. Its next flush uses that receipt and preserves the covered member’s durable repeat deadline. A partial group-log update retains previously acknowledged siblings still present in the same state. Only members actually selected for
provider entry receive a claim. Prior confirmed terminal contacts may be
elided without admitting members beyond the original prefix. An ordinary
firing copy cannot consume an expired source's clear. A pending reservation
checks frozen source labels, annotations, generator URL, exact times, managed
identity, and wire resolution state across retries and owner replacement. Uncommissioned adapters leave ordinary routing, inhibition
and notification readers on the existing native pipeline. An active reader
rejects a reload to unsupported destinations. The full transport set and the
inhibition publication barrier remain prerequisites for commissioning.

Measure three separate workloads. A proven untracked direct destination should
add no journal HTTP or SQL operation on the negative path. First canonical
binding pays for durable native identity creation. Repeated canonical no-policy
traffic includes lifecycle, delivery and coverage costs. D1–D3 must keep this
cohort in the same-work before/after latency comparison and meet the existing
5% threshold. Measure a 150-minute loaded interval plus drain. Timings from
static inspection or source compilation cannot establish these properties.
